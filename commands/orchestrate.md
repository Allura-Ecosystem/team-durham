# Brand Orchestration Mode

Apply `governance/adaptive-workflow.md` and `contracts/adaptive-routing.json`.
Use the user's active client/project; do not silently choose another client's data.
Read that client's approved brief, strategy, and source packs. Obtain or reuse the
Scout ContextPacket; Brain unavailable means disclosed local-only hydration.

1. Kotler routes quick/standard/sensitive and assigns one accountable producer.
2. Aaker validates intent and STP; lock strategy before creative production.
3. Ogilvy produces naming/copy; Glaser owns visual direction. Independent copy
   and visual preparation may parallelize after approved inputs. Name-dependent
   logo work waits for the approved name. Keep artifact ownership disjoint.
4. Rand assembles the requested kit from verified source packs.
5. Select independent read-only review by risk under the adaptive policy; the
   producing role fixes findings. Verify each artifact before dependent work.
6. Log substantive verified outcomes as Brain traces with
   `group_id: allura-team-durham`. Read back writes before claiming persistence.
   Semantic promotion belongs to a human curator. Report missing/failed tools;
   never fall back to direct database writes.
7. Report artifacts, checks, route, owner/reviewer, terminal state, and next action.

Do not ask to continue at routine phase transitions within approved scope.
Human approval remains required for shipping, client sending, strategy changes,
and promotion; existing explicit authorization counts. Stop only affected work
at a missing approval, genuine blocker, or bounded budget exhaustion.

For status, compare current artifacts with available Brain evidence and identify
unknowns. Reuse the scoped packet; refresh changed dependencies and authority.
